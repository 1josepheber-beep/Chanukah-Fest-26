Thirty-one years of light at City Hall

The menorah has gone up outside Houston City Hall every Chanukah since 1995. This year it happens on the third night.

What began as a public lighting has grown into one of the largest Jewish community gatherings in Greater Houston, drawing families, business leaders and elected officials from across the region. It is open to everyone — Jewish or not, affiliated or not.

Chanukah Fest 2026 falls on Sunday, December 6, the third night of Chanukah. The afternoon runs as a festival: live music, more than twenty activity and craft booths, kosher food, strolling performers, a digital raffle and a toy drive. At six o'clock everything stops for the ceremony, and the evening closes with fireworks behind the Grand Menorah.

Admission is free. Food, merchandise and VIP seating are available for purchase.
