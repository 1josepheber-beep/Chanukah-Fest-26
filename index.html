Lighting up Houston for 31 years

Since 1995, the Grand Menorah at Houston City Hall has stood as a radiant beacon of hope, freedom, and unity across Greater Houston. On Sunday, December 6, 2026—the third night of Chanukah—Houston Chanukah Fest will once again unite every corner of our city in a shared spirit of light, pride, and community, welcoming everyone regardless of background or affiliation.

At 3pm a festival packed with live music, over twenty interactive craft and activity booths, Kosher food, strolling street performers, raffle, and a community toy drive. At 6pm, the Menorah Lighting Ceremony alongside civic leaders, culminating in a spectacular fireworks display illuminating the downtown sky.

This landmark celebration is hosted by Chabad Outreach of Houston, a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to providing spiritual guidance, community outreach, and inspiring educational programs that connect Jews across Greater Houston to their shared heritage.

Before you come

Do I need a ticket to attend?

Admission is 100% FREE and open to all. General entry costs nothing - you only pay if you choose to purchase food, festival merchandise, or upgraded Reserved Seating.

Parking & transportation
  • Free street parking — Metered street parking in Downtown Houston is free on Sundays.
  • Guaranteed garage parking — Reserve a parking space in advance at the Theater District Garage (Tranquility 2), 499 Rusk St., Houston, TX 77002. Reserve your garage space
  • Rideshare & carpooling — To avoid traffic and limited street spots, rideshare (Uber/Lyft) or carpooling is encouraged.

How does Reserved Seating work?

A Reserved Seating ticket guarantees a dedicated seat in the front section facing the main stage for the entire show, complete with full in-and-out privileges all afternoon.

Should I pre-register?

Help us keep Houston Chanukah Fest safe and smooth for all families by registering in advance. Plus, every pre-registered guest is automatically entered into our Grand Chanukah Free Raffle! 🎟️✨ 👉 Register here

Is it good for young children?

Yes. A large share of the booths are hands-on crafts and activities built for kids, and strolling performers work the crowd all afternoon.

What is the Bag Policy?

A strict No Bag Policy is enforced at our Smith Street checkpoint. Prohibited: Backpacks, luggage, oversized totes, and large purses. Permitted: Small clutches, diaper bags, and medical supply bags (all subject to security search). Thank you for helping us keep this celebration safe and joyful for all families!

Is the food kosher?

Yes, everything sold on site is kosher.
